FREQUENT QUESTIONS

How long does shipping take?

Standard local delivery across Metro Manila takes 2 to 3 business days, while provincial and hotel deliveries across the Philippines take 4 to 7 business days.

Can I return a shirt?

Yes! Unworn garments with original tags attached can be returned or exchanged for a different size within 7 days of delivery.

Where can I view the size guide?

You can check our detailed Filipino garment measurement chart directly on each product page under the Shop tab.
