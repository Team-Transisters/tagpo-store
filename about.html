About

Tagpo turns the Philippines' beauty into everyday streetwear. Wear Filipino pride.

THE TAGPO STORY

Tagpo was created to serve both Filipinos and tourists visiting the Philippines. The word tagpo means "meeting point"—the intersection where Filipino culture and daily style meet. We believe cultural clothing belongs beyond traditional occasions. It should be lived in, worn boldly, and integrated seamlessly into your everyday wardrobe.

BEHIND THE DESIGN

Every drop we release tells a story of a specific province. We take inspiration from local events, historic landmarks, regional comfort foods, and iconic national symbols. Our design process transforms these elements into modern graphic concepts made for a global audience. Look closely at our prints, and you see a curated piece of the Philippines.

OUR VALUES

We honor rooted heritage and heavy-duty quality by focusing on traditional craftsmanship and regional stories instead of fleeting fashion trends. Built with trusted materials and precise designs, Tagpo creates long-lasting garments that serve as a wearable bridge to the Philippines' culture.

WEAR YOUR PRIDE

Our culture is constantly evolving, and now it has a new way to be worn. Explore our collections, find the pieces that represent your roots or honor your journey, and wear them with absolute confidence.
