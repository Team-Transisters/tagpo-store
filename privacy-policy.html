Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we store and protect it, and how it is utilized when you browse and shop on our traditional clothing platform.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a smooth shopping experience for travelers and customers exploring our traditional Filipino attire, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identification: Name, email address, phone number, and delivery/shipping addresses provided during checkout or account registration.
  • Payment Details: Billing information and payment confirmation status collected securely through our payment gateway partners.
  • Browsing & Device Information: IP addresses, browser types, device specifications, and usage data collected automatically as you view our catalog, interact with 3D product models, or navigate site pages.
  • Communication Data: Inquiries, feedback, or messages submitted through our Contact Form, direct phone calls, or official social media channels.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data solely for specific and legitimate operational purposes:

  • Fulfillment & Delivery: Processing orders, managing payments, and delivering garments to your home, temporary travel accommodation, or hotel across the Philippines.
  • Interactive Features: Enhancing website functionality, including performance optimization for our 3D interactive garment viewers.
  • Customer Support: Responding to inquiries submitted via our contact channels or resolving order-related issues.
  • Updates & Promotions: Sending transactional notifications (e.g., order confirmations) and optional brand updates regarding new heritage collections, provided you have opted in.

3. How We Share Your Data

We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal data. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers necessary to operate our platform:

  • Payment Processors: Secure third-party gateways that handle credit card or digital wallet transactions.
  • Logistics & Courier Partners: Delivery services responsible for dispatching and delivering your orders.
  • Hosting & IT Infrastructure: Web servers and database management tools used to host and protect our platform.
  • Legal Compliance: Authorities or legal entities if required by law, regulation, or court order.

4. Data Security and Retention

  • Connection Security: We use industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) to scramble sensitive data during transmission between your browser and our server.
  • Data Protection: We maintain administrative and technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or modification of stored personal data.
  • Retention: We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill order requests, comply with tax or legal obligations, or resolve active support inquiries.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes cookies and similar tracking tools to recognize your browser, keep track of items in your shopping cart, and evaluate website traffic performance. You can choose to disable cookies through your web browser settings, though some interactive site features may not function fully as a result.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Access, review, or request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Request the deletion of your account or personal data, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Opt out of receiving marketing or promotional emails at any time using the "Unsubscribe" link included in our communications.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in site operations, features, or relevant legal regulations. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices or your personal data, please reach out via our contact channels:

  • Contact Form: Available on our Contact page
  • Customer Support Phone: Listed on our official Contact page
  • Official Social Media Profiles: Accessible via our website footer or visit through the contact page.
